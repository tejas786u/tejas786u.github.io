Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Your progress, bookmarks and quiz scores are saved on your device.
Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
Yes. Progress is stored only on your device, so deleting the app removes it.
How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Open the More menu (⋯) on the Learn tab and choose Privacy Settings. This option appears where privacy rules require it, such as in Europe.
The programs say "C17". Will they work with my compiler?
Yes. Every program is standard C and compiles with modern GCC or Clang, e.g. gcc -std=c17 program.c.
